# Customer guide

- **Sign in** with the username and password from your email. Forgot it? Tap *Forgot password?* — if your account has an email, you get a link.
- **Home** shows your subscription, *Continue watching* and recently watched channels.
- **Live / Movies / Series**: pick a category, search, tap ★ to add a favourite.
- **Player**: subtitles (CC), audio (🎧), quality (⚙️), picture-in-picture and fullscreen where your device supports them.
- **Account**: your plan and expiry, *My devices* (sign out a device you no longer use), *Support requests* (write to us and read the answer), *Payments* (receipts you can print), *Parental controls* (hide adult categories behind a 4-digit PIN on this device), language and password.
- **Get the app**: the download page has steps for Android, Android TV / Fire TV, iPhone, Windows and Mac.
- **TV guide**: in *Live*, switch from *Channels* to *TV guide* to see what is on now and next for the category.
- **My Details** (Account): add your own provider with Xtream Codes or an M3U playlist. The details stay on your device.
- **Two-step sign-in** and **Verify email** (Account): extra protection for your account.
- **Support requests** can include one picture per message.
- **Chat with support**: the round chat button opens a live conversation with us.

## Referrals, gifts & lifetime plans (20 Nov 2026 update)
- **Refer a friend or family member** (Account → *Referral*): share your personal link. When they buy an eligible 1-year subscription you get **1 month free**; if they choose the **2-device 1-year ($109.99)** plan you get **3 months free**, and for the **4-device 1-year ($139.99)** plan you get **6 months free**. The free days are added to your own subscription after their purchase; your code cannot be used on your own account, and one code stays attached to each account.
- **Gifts & discounts**: all offers are shown together in one simple place — *1 month free, 3 months free, 6 months free* or *10% / 20% discount*. Reseller accounts only see discounts (10% or 20%), never free months.
- **Lifetime Intproto-TV**: **Lifetime Standard (1 device) $499.95** and **Lifetime VIP (4 devices) $999.95**. These are fixed prices across every package and promotion, with no extra discount or referral months.
- **On a phone or tablet**: tap **☰ Menu** to reach Account → Referral. The panel and guide use the same colours and simple one-column layout on iPhone, Samsung, Android and tablets, so the referral link and gift list are easy to read and copy.
