# Customer guide

- **Sign in** with the username and password from your email. Forgot it? Tap *Forgot password?* — if your account has an email, you get a link.
- **Home** shows your subscription, *Continue watching* and recently watched channels.
- **Live / Movies / Series**: pick a category, search, tap ★ to add a favourite.
- **Player**: subtitles (CC), audio (🎧), quality (⚙️), picture-in-picture and fullscreen where your device supports them.
- **Account**: your plan and expiry, *My devices* (sign out a device you no longer use), *Support requests* (write to us and read the answer), *Payments* (receipts you can print), *Parental controls* (hide adult categories behind a 4-digit PIN on this device), language and password.
- **Get the app**: the download page has steps for Android, Android TV / Fire TV, iPhone, Windows and Mac.
